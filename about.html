About

The systems behind the idea.

I'm Angel Simcha, a product builder, operator and responsible AI leader working at the intersection of technology, education and community infrastructure in Africa.

I started in operations: managing communities, running the backend of programmes, and coordinating initiatives that moved thousands of young people through digital and financial education, with EmpowerHer, Women in DeFi, Uniscope and Konoha. That work taught me one thing. Most initiatives collapse at the system level, not the idea level. So I moved from running programmes to designing the structures underneath them.

Today I am Co-founder, COO and Responsible AI Lead at Hammet, where I lead product, curriculum and operations for an AI Career Pathway Platform for young Africans. I also founded Operators Corner, a leadership development platform that helps emerging leaders execute with clarity, and I co-lead WittyLTD and Konoha University. Over five years I have been part of more than 14 ventures.

Learning is a practice, not a phase. I hold the Google Project Management Certificate, have completed 40+ professional certifications and 50+ executive development programmes, and apply AI ethics and governance training directly to the products I build.

I am a final-year Psychology student at the University of Lagos and a YALI Fellow. My faith shapes how I lead, how I build and what I build toward. Outside of building, I read and I write.
