Full (about 150 words)

Angel Nwafor (popularly known as Angel Simcha) is an operations leader, entrepreneur and Certified Christian Professional Counsellor building across AI, leadership and faith-centered impact.

She is Co-Founder and COO of HammetLabs (AI literacy for Nigerian students), where she also oversees product, and Co-Founder of WittyLTD. She founded Operators Corner, a leadership platform that has empowered 1,000+ professionals, Zoe Inc., a faith-and-execution platform impacting hundreds, and Zoe Bookstore. Her work has touched thousands through Women in DeFi, Empower Her Community and Konoha University.

A trained facilitator and speaker, she has taught hundreds in leadership and project management through retreats and corporate trainings, backed by 50+ executive development programmes completed.

She is a final-year Psychology student at the University of Lagos, proof that scale and youth are not mutually exclusive.
