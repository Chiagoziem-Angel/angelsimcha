Full (about 170 words)

Angel Simcha is a product builder, operator and responsible AI leader working at the intersection of technology, education and community infrastructure in Africa. She is Co-founder, Chief Operating Officer and Responsible AI Lead at Hammet, an AI Career Pathway Platform for young Africans, where she leads product, curriculum and operations. She also founded Operators Corner, a leadership development platform with a mission to equip 1 million leaders with the systems, skills and clarity to lead with excellence.

Angel co-founded Konoha University and WittyLTD, serves as Operations Manager of the Konoha Financial Community, and served as Head of Operations for the Lagos AI Career Experience. Over five years she has been part of more than 14 ventures spanning education, finance, media and community. She is a YALI Fellow, holds the Google Project Management Certificate, is a Certified Christian Professional Counsellor (CCPC), and is completing a degree in Psychology at the University of Lagos. Her faith shapes how she leads, builds and serves.
